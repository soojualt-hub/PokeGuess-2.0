    How to play

    1. A random Pokémon appears as a silhouette with one clue.
    2. Type your guess and press Enter. Each wrong guess unlocks a clue.
    3. After 5 wrong guesses, Reveal Pokémon unlocks.
    4. Fewer wrong guesses earn more points.

    About

    PokéGuess is a class project that tests your Pokémon knowledge. Names, images and stats come from PokéAPI. Pokémon is a trademark of Nintendo, Game Freak and Creatures Inc.; this is a fan project.

    Made for fans, gamers, students and casual players.
