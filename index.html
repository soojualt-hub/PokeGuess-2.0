    How to play

    1. A random Pokémon appears as a silhouette with one clue.
    2. Type your guess and press Enter. Each wrong guess unlocks a clue.
    3. After 5 wrong guesses, Reveal Pokémon unlocks.
    4. Fewer wrong guesses earn more points.
    5. Your progress is saved under your name, so you can load it later.
